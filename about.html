About WSIDN

What Should I Do Now is an independent information service for people in Pakistan who need a clear next step when something goes wrong.

What we do

  • Publish short, practical guides for common problems.
  • Point to official help pages — never unofficial copies.
  • Warn about common scams and mistakes.
  • Correct mistakes when we find them.

What we don't do

  • We never ask for your CNIC number, password, OTP, or bank details.
  • We don't sell or rank results based on sponsors.
  • We don't give legal or medical diagnoses — for emergencies, call 1122.

Corrections & reports

Report an outdated link or a wrong helpline using the feedback buttons on the guide page. High-stakes guides are prioritised.

Independence

WSIDN is free to read and may show clearly-labelled ads in the future. Advertisers do not influence which next steps a guide recommends.

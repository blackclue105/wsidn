Editorial policy

1. Research

Each guide is written from primary sources: the relevant authority's official website, official franchise or helpline documentation, and the provider's published procedures. We do not copy text from other blogs.

2. Verification

Official links are opened and checked before publication. Helpline numbers, fees, and deadlines are only listed when they appear on an official source at the time of the last verification.

3. Uncertainty

Where a procedure depends on a provider, province, or circumstances, we say so explicitly instead of guessing.

4. Last verified date

Every guide shows a Last verified date. That date reflects the last real check of the links and steps — it is never invented.

5. Corrections

Reported issues are triaged. High-stakes guides (scams, documents, emergencies) are prioritised. Corrections are reflected in the guide and in the "Last verified" date.

6. Advertisers

If ads are shown, they are clearly labelled. Emergency instructions are never replaced by, or interleaved with, ad units that could look like official buttons.

7. Feedback

Each guide includes Solved / Still stuck buttons. These signals are stored locally in your browser in v1 and help prioritise which guides need a rewrite.

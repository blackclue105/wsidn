Privacy

WSIDN is built to collect as little about you as possible.

What we collect

  • Nothing you type into the search box is sent to a server — search runs in your browser.
  • Guide feedback (Solved / Still stuck) is stored only in your browser's local storage.
  • If ads are enabled in future, the ad provider's own cookie policy will apply; we will list providers here.

What we never ask for

  • Your CNIC number, bank details, OTP, or password.
  • A photo of any identity document.

Third-party services

We load fonts from Google Fonts. Opening official links leaves this site and takes you to the provider's own website, where their privacy policy applies.
